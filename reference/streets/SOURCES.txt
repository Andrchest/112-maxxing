Coverage notes (E23 recon; not decided here, Q-E11-2/Q-E23-1..4):
- 45242 ways, 5629 unique names; no okrug/district column (way tags only — a district would need
  a spatial join with admin boundaries, not attempted).
- The official ОМК УМ classifier (data.mos.ru, classifier 2044) needs a registered apidata.mos.ru
  api_key (HTTP 403 without one) — an owner action, not fetched (Q-E23-1).
- КЛАДР / ФИАС are other official-form sources; ФИАС's `gar_xml.zip` is ~57.7 GB with no regional
  subset and was not fetched; the КЛАДР `base.7z` (61 MB) was noted but is outside E23's brief.
- Both «Дубнинская улица» and «Дубининская улица» are real Moscow streets in this list — a
  directory lookup alone cannot tell which one a caller meant (Q-E23-3).

Not a WorldTruth source and not scoring data (INV 3): a report-only reference list, read at report
time only (D35), on the 112 card path only. Absent this directory, the report section says
«Проверка недоступна». Street names in the scenarios are never changed to match this list — see
D-h and `docs/hld/00-decisions.md` D35 on `scenarios/tickets/ticket-15-call-3`'s «ул. Зверенецкая».
